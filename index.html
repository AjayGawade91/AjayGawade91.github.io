02 · Verification

How I verify accuracy

An end-to-end accuracy chain, checked against ground truth that is independent of the system under test. Select a stage to see what is measured.

Independent ground truth

Fiducials appear in the CT volume, the laser scan and the tracker frame. Because they are shared, any two frames can be compared without trusting the third, so registration error is measured, not assumed.
