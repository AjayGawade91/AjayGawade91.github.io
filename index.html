About

I work on image-guided and force-aware surgical robots. My projects at Johns Hopkins span orthopedic and ENT procedures, the da Vinci Research Kit, and magnetic microrobots.

My approach is simple: build the system, then check it against an independent ground truth, such as a laser scanner, an optical tracker or a force sensor. Every result on this page comes from that kind of comparison.
